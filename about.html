About Castro Valley Adult and Career Education's

Medical Academy

Castro Valley Adult and Career Education (CVACE) is a cornerstone of workforce development in the East Bay, empowering adult learners with the skills, credentials, and confidence to launch and advance meaningful careers. Founded on the belief that education should be accessible to everyone—regardless of age or background—CVACE serves hundreds of students each year through a wide range of career-technical programs designed in close partnership with local employers and industry leaders.

Our Medical Academy stands at the heart of what we do. Through hands-on and hybrid learning pathways, we prepare adults for in-demand roles across the healthcare industry. Programs such as the Clinical Medical Assistant, Phlebotomy Technician, and Community Health Worker tracks combine rigorous classroom instruction with real-world clinical experience, ensuring graduates are job-ready from day one. Our state-of-the-art training facilities replicate authentic healthcare environments, giving students the practical exposure they need to thrive.

At CVACE, we understand that our students often balance work, family, and other responsibilities alongside their education. That is why our programs are offered in flexible formats—including evening, weekend, and online options—so that pursuing a new career never means putting your life on hold. Our dedicated advisors work one-on-one with each student to map out a personalized pathway to certification, employment, and long-term career growth.

Beyond the classroom, CVACE connects students directly to the regional healthcare job market through employer partnerships, career fairs, and externship placements at leading medical facilities throughout Alameda County and the greater Bay Area. Whether you are seeking your first healthcare role, returning to the workforce, or looking to earn a new certification to advance your career, CVACE provides the tools, expertise, and community support you need to succeed. Our instructors bring real-world industry experience into every session, and our graduates consistently go on to build rewarding careers in clinics, hospitals, and community health organizations across the region.
